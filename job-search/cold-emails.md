## Tips for sending

- **Personalize the first 2 sentences** for each company — reference something specific (a blog post, a product, a job description).
- **Find the right person**: engineering manager, head of platform, or a senior engineer on the team you want to join — not HR.
- **LinkedIn message version**: cut each email to 3 short paragraphs. Remove the sign-off block, keep the GitHub link.
- **Follow up once** after 5–7 days if no response. One follow-up is professional; two is too many.
- **Add your GitHub link before sending** — replace all `[your-github-url]` placeholders.
